# Sky assets

## starmap-2020-1k.jpg

**Deep Star Maps 2020** — NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific
Visualization Studio. Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851

All-sky equirectangular map in EQUATORIAL coordinates, built from Hipparcos-2,
Tycho-2 and Gaia DR2. Downsampled from the published 4K print release.

Chosen over the more obvious ESO Milky Way panorama specifically because that
one is in GALACTIC coordinates: rotating it until the band looks right over a
scene is an aesthetic placement dressed up as an astronomical one. This map is
rotated by the actual local sidereal time of the moment the scene depicts
(2026-08-20, 22:19 at 47.886 N, 106.906 E) and lands where it lands.

This credit must ship with the site. See `lib/starmap.ts`.
